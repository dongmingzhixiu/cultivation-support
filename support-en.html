Support

GPS activities and route replay

In Training, open Record track · Photos, choose Walk, Running, or Cycling, then tap Start Training and allow Location when prompted. If location is unavailable, find Cultivation in iOS Settings and check its location permission. To continue recording while your screen is locked, check the system's background location setting when prompted. If the online basemap does not load, the app can still draw the route locally.

Exporting activities and deleting photos

Use the activity log's export option that includes photos to back up both routes and photos. Before changing devices or reinstalling, check that the backup is saved outside the app. If you delete an activity, its photos are moved to the unassigned photos section, where you can delete them individually.

Webpage import and reminders

Paste a public article link in the study library. If the site blocks access, you can paste text you have permission to use. If a reminder does not appear, check the reminder setting in the app and the notification permission in iOS Settings.

Leaderboards, sync and deletion

Open Methods → Device identity & leaderboards, create a device identity, and confirm joining each board separately. Realm, Meditation Time and Training Distance are off by default. Joining syncs current settled totals, including eligible manual and imported records; tied scores share a rank. An ongoing session counts after it ends. Your private name stays on your device; boards show a system name and a built-in avatar.

If sync fails, check your connection and select Retry sync. Leaving a board removes only that board’s score. Delete leaderboard identity removes your server profile and all board scores while preserving local records. If a request is pending, retry online and wait for confirmation before uninstalling. This version has no email/password sign-in or cross-device recovery; ordinary backups exclude leaderboard identity. Contact support if your old device is lost or your listing is paused. Do not send passwords, identity credentials or complete routes.
