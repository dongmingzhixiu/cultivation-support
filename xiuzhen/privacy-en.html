Information stored on your device

Cultivation does not require an app account. It stores your focus and activity records, learning progress, text and notes you enter or import, photos, and settings on your device. The app uses these to show your progress, replay routes, and continue where you left off. If you add a Home Screen widget, the app and widget share the progress information needed for that display on your device.

When you start a GPS activity, the app uses system location services to record your route, calculate distance, and associate photos with locations. An ongoing activity can continue recording while the screen is locked or the app is in the background, subject to your system permissions. End the activity to stop location recording for that session. When you take or choose a photo, the app processes the selected image and saves a copy with your activity. When you take an activity photo, the app can request a location fix to place the photo on the route, subject to system permissions. If you select read aloud, the app uses iOS speech synthesis to read the text. If you enable reminders, the app uses local notifications at the time you set.

Optional leaderboards and device identity

You may create a device identity, choose a built-in avatar, and set a private name stored only on this device. Realm, total meditation time and total training distance leaderboards are all off by default. Creating a device identity alone does not register it with the server. After you confirm joining an individual board, we transmit and retain your random public ID, system name ID, built-in avatar ID and current score for that board, plus authentication hashes, revisions and synchronization times. Scores for other boards, daily records, GPS points, photos, study content and your private name are not uploaded to the leaderboard server. Scores are linked to your random ID, so this is not described as anonymous data.

Participants can see your system name, built-in avatar, selected scores and rank. There is no chat, custom public text or uploaded avatar. Leaving a board removes its score. Deleting your leaderboard identity removes the server profile and all scores while preserving local practice data. If a request cannot be confirmed, it stays pending and you may remain listed. Non-public ID and double-hashed credential revocation digests remain until the service ends to prevent replayed requests from recreating a deleted identity; original secrets and deleted scores are not retained in these digests.

The device credential is protected by system secure storage and excluded from ordinary exports. Identity and participation choices are not restored by ordinary imports. There is no account sign-in recovery or migration to another device. Uninstalling does not delete server data; delete the leaderboard identity in settings first. Contact the operator for assistance if the device is lost. The leaderboard service is hosted on Tencent Cloud in Silicon Valley, United States, and data is transmitted to the United States for processing. Administrators may pause or restore public listings, or delete a leaderboard identity to handle user requests or review data; these actions do not directly change your device records. Management records contain an irreversible target-ID digest, administrator, action category and time, without private names or scores. The interface shows the most recent 30 days; older records are removed during subsequent management operations or queries. Transfers use HTTPS, database access is restricted, and management access requires authentication. Data protection rules may differ from those where you live; these technical measures do not imply any particular cross-border certification. Restricted database snapshots may be created for deployment maintenance. Deletion does not automatically erase each existing snapshot; the latest withdrawals, deletions and revocations must be applied before restoring service so deleted identities are not republished.

Connections to external services

  • Online maps. When you open a route replay that contains locations, your device may request map tiles directly from Esri ArcGIS. Requests contain zoom levels and tile row and column coordinates, which can indicate the area shown around your route or photos. Esri also receives network request information such as your IP address. Cultivation draws the route and photo markers on your device. Map requests do not contain your full list of GPS points, photo files, or study records. Esri's privacy statement describes logging access information such as IP addresses. The recipient is Esri (Environmental Systems Research Institute, Inc. and relevant affiliates). We do not control its log retention and have not obtained an endpoint-specific retention period. Its applicable policies and terms govern processing. Contact privacy@esri.com for questions or requests for access, correction or deletion. Esri Privacy Statement
  • Importing or opening webpages. When you enter a link in the study library and choose to read or open it, your device contacts the website you selected and any destination it redirects to. The site receives request information such as your IP address. In the in-app viewer, the site and the third-party resources it loads may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies. Imported text is stored in your on-device library when you save it.
  • Sharing and backups you create. You can choose to export your records to a file or use the system share sheet. An activity export with photos can include route coordinates, timestamps, and photos. Recipients and storage services you choose handle those copies under their own policies. Deleting records in the app does not remove exported copies or copies others received.
  • Contacting support. When you contact us, the operator uses your contact details and the information you choose to provide to respond, investigate the issue, or handle your privacy request. Support correspondence and related case materials are retained for up to 180 days after the issue is resolved. You may request earlier deletion at the contact address above. Material required by law is retained only to the extent and for the period required.

Managing permissions and deleting data

Use Settings on your iPhone or iPad to change location, camera, photo, and notification permissions. Changes affect future use of those permissions. Existing records can still be managed in the app.

You can delete activity records and photos from the activity log. Deleting an activity moves its photos to the unassigned photos section, where you can delete them individually. You can delete works in the study library. Local app data remains until you delete the relevant items or use the system's Delete App action to remove the app and its local data. Manage device backups, original library photos, and exported copies separately. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent to the operator, or ask about third-party requests, email the privacy contact above.
